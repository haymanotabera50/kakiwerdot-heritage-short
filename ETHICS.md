# ETHICS: Consent, Cultural Representation, and AI Disclosure

## 1. Informed Consent & Likeness Policy

- **No Living Individual's Likeness Used:** This production does **not** simulate, replicate, or use the facial likeness, biometric features, or vocal recordings of any living person.
- **No Private Individuals Portrayed:** All visual and vocal assets are generated using synthetic models guided by culturally respectful descriptive anchors.
- **Historical Persona Representation:** The central character, Kakiwerdot, is based on Gurage oral history and Ethiopian theatrical memory (*Kake Werdwot*). Because documented archival records are limited, she is ethically presented as **a living cultural symbol of resilience**, rather than claiming verifiable historical biographical quotes.

---

## 2. Cultural Representation & Respectful Heritage Safeguards

1. **Anti-Stereotyping Guidelines:**
   - The film explicitly avoids generic Hollywood or Pan-Africanist tropes (e.g. West African Kente cloths or turbans).
   - Visual prompts specifically feature verified Gurage cultural elements: traditional round thatched architecture with woven bamboo interior work (*Gurage Bet*), the false banana plant (*Enset*), and traditional highland Ethiopian handwoven *Kemis* with *tibeb* geometric borders.
2. **Community Representation:**
   - Traditional elders and community members are not portrayed antagonistically or maliciously. The story honors Ethiopian customary tradition while celebrating the essential role of women's wisdom, dignity (*Kibr*), and education (*Iwqet*).
3. **Language Verification:**
   - The primary narration is presented in authentic Amharic with accurate cultural syntax, accompanied by accurate English subtitles. Unverified pseudo-Guragigna phrases were deliberately excluded to maintain linguistic authenticity.
